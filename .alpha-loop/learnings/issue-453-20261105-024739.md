- Daemon and factory startup reject live competing locks with PID and lock-path diagnostics; stale competing locks are preserved and reported once.

## What Failed
- Startup exclusion and stale-lock behavior were initially undocumented; review corrected README.md and docs/factory-mode.md.

## Patterns
- Acquire the coordinator’s own lock before checking the competing lock; release it if startup is refused.

## Anti-Patterns
- Changing startup behavior without documenting refusal conditions, diagnostics, and flag exceptions.
