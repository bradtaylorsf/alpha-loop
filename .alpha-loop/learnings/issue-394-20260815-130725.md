## What Worked
- Implemented explicit telemetry provenance so reported and estimated tokens/costs are distinguishable, with unknown pricing rendered as `n/a`.

## What Failed
- Initial implementation left the bundled zero-tool-call `err_rate` unit bug unresolved.

## Patterns
- Measurement provenance should travel with every metric; missing, estimated, reported, and explicitly free values must remain distinct through aggregation and rendering.

## Anti-Patterns
- Do not use numeric zero as a fallback for unknown pricing or unavailable measurements.
